Career reality

AI, automation & layoffs: what should you know?

Layoffs have many causes, and AI is only one factor in some cases. No course can guarantee a job—but continuous learning and strong fundamentals can improve your ability to adapt.

Remember: Don't learn AI out of fear. Learn it to become better at identifying problems, building solutions, and working with people.
01

Strengthen fundamentals

Practice programming, data structures, databases, statistics, and clear communication alongside AI tools.

02

Adapt instead of chasing hype

Understand what AI can and cannot do. Verify outputs, protect sensitive data, and learn to evaluate quality.

03

Show what you can build

Publish projects, explain trade-offs, document your work, and seek internships or collaborative experience.
